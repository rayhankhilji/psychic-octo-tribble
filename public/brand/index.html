Logo

The mark and the wordmark

An isometric city block on a sand tile, with a Golden Gate–orange tower and the sun behind it. The wordmark is Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold, outlined, so the files need no fonts. Use the files as they are; please don’t redraw, recolour or rearrange them.

Clear space

Leave the height of the “T” free on every side. For the mark alone, a quarter of its width.

Smallest sizes

  • Horizontal logo: 24 px tall (6 mm in print).
  • Stacked logo: 48 px tall.
  • Mark: 16 px; one-colour mark: 32 px.
  • Smaller than that, use the mark alone.

Please don’t

  • Recolour, stretch, tilt or outline the logo.
  • Retype the wordmark, or write it “Tiny Town”.
  • Add glows, bevels or gradients.
  • Place it on a busy part of a photo. Find calm sky or water, or use a panel.
