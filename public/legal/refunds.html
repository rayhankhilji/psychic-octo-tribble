Refund Policy

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE] · Last updated 13 October 2026

We don’t refund subscription payments. In return, we make cancelling easy, you keep everything you’ve paid for, and if we get your billing wrong, we put it right in full.

The short version

  • No refunds on subscription payments, including partly used months or years.
  • Cancel any time, in a couple of clicks. You won’t be charged again, and your city stays open until the end of the period you’ve paid for.
  • If we charged you twice, or charged you by mistake, we refund it in full.
  • Your rights under consumer law always come first.

1Why there are no refunds

TinyTown has no free trial, but the demo is free: you can explore a grown city and see exactly how it works before you pay. Once you subscribe, you’re never committed beyond the month or year you’ve paid for: you can cancel whenever you like and keep your city until that period ends.

2How to cancel

  1. Open TinyTown and go to Account.
  2. Choose Manage billing. This opens Stripe’s billing portal.
  3. Choose Cancel plan and confirm.

Your plan won’t renew. Your city stays open until the end of the current month or year, then your plan ends. We keep your city, so if you subscribe again later you pick up where you left off. If you’d rather we didn’t keep it, delete your account.

Renewals are charged at the start of each new period, so cancel before your renewal date to avoid the next charge. The billing portal shows that date.

3When we do refund

  • Duplicate charges. If you were charged twice for the same period, we refund the extra charge in full.
  • Billing errors. If you were charged the wrong amount, charged after you cancelled or deleted your account, or charged for a plan you didn’t choose, we refund what we shouldn’t have taken.
  • Where the law requires it, including the rights described below.

4If you’re in the EU or the UK

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom and subscribe as a consumer (not for your business), the law may give you 14 days from the day you subscribe to change your mind.

If the right applies to you, email us within those 14 days and we’ll refund your payment, less an amount in proportion to the days you’ve already had where the law allows that.

In some cases the law ends this right once your access has started, if you asked for it to start straight away and agreed that you’d lose the right.

Wherever you live, nothing in this policy takes away rights you have under consumer law in your country.

5How to ask for a refund

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address you sign in with. Tell us the date and amount of the charge (or forward the receipt) and what went wrong. We reply within two business days.

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Stripe. They usually show up within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

6Before you dispute a charge

Please email us first. We fix genuine mistakes quickly, and it’s much faster than a bank dispute. If a charge is disputed with your bank, we may pause your account until the dispute is settled.
